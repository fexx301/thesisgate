# Practitioner validation sheet

This is a preparation artifact only. No outreach or tester claim is made.

## Participant target

Five relevant rToken or crypto-native retail traders, ideally people who place 1,000–10,000 USDT discretionary long spot trades and can read an order-book snapshot. Record only a participant code and the consented task result; do not collect account credentials or thesis text by default.

## Ten-minute task

1. Give the participant one randomized, unlabeled ThesisGate decision card, one strong general-AI brief, and one calculator-only output built from the same source/market packet.
2. Ask them to explain, in 90 seconds, what the source does and does not establish, what the scenario requires after costs, and what could change each conclusion.
3. Ask which output they would use for a second thesis and why.
4. Record task duration, whether both conclusions were correctly explained, preferred output, and the specific source/time/cost boundary they named.

## Predeclared targets

- At least 4/5 explain both conclusions within 90 seconds.
- At least 3/5 prefer the two-conclusion card and name a specific boundary that changed their decision.
- Zero material source or arithmetic errors in the shown cards.

Report the denominator and all failures. If participants cannot be reached by the cutoff, report zero user validation rather than replacing it with developer intuition or invented usage metrics.
